# Code of conduct

Agent Toolkit welcomes participation from people of all backgrounds and experience
levels. This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other interactions
in project-managed spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and communicate professionally.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and remain open to correction.
- Help newcomers understand the context and expectations of a contribution.
- Respect privacy and share another person's information only with permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and
deliberate intimidation are unacceptable. This includes publishing private
information without consent, repeated disruptive behavior, and retaliation
against someone who raises a concern.

## Raising a concern

Ask a repository maintainer to review conduct that conflicts with these standards.
For concerns visible in an issue or pull request, flag the interaction without
repeating abusive content or exposing private information.

If a concern needs private discussion and no private contact has been established,
[open an issue](https://github.com/QuinntyneBrown/agent-toolkit/issues/new) containing
only a request for a private conversation with a maintainer. Share the details
after a private channel is agreed. For content that violates GitHub's rules,
GitHub's reporting tools are also available.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may request a change in behavior, remove inappropriate content, close
a discussion, or restrict participation according to the severity and recurrence
of the behavior. Decisions should consider the available context and protect
participants from further disruption.

Concerns about a moderation decision can be raised with a maintainer, including
any additional context that may change the decision.
